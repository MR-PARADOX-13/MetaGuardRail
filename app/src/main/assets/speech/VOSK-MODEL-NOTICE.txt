Speech model notice

Speech models are downloaded over HTTPS on first use and stored in app-private storage. They are
not bundled in the APK. Both models are from Alpha Cephei Inc. and are listed under the Apache
License 2.0 on the official Vosk model page. See VOSK-LICENSE.txt.

Model page: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
Model metadata copyright: Copyright 2020 Alpha Cephei Inc

The English model archive is about 40 MB. The Telugu model archive is about 58 MB. Both models
remain on the device for offline use after download.

The Telugu model's published Fleurs benchmark reports an 87.9 word error rate; Telugu playback
detection is experimental and may miss speech.

English model: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models/vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15.zip
Telugu model: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models/vosk-model-small-te-0.42.zip
